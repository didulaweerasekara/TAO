How every programme works

The TAO Method, in every room.

Whatever the subject, programmes follow the same sequence, so that learning becomes behaviour.

  1. Assess

    Understand the situation, the context, strengths and blind spots.

  2. Learn

    Frameworks, models and concepts that make the situation clearer.

  3. Practise

    Simulations, role play, exercises, discussion and feedback.

  4. Apply

    Specific actions and behaviours to take back to work.
