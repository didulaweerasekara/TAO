The TAO Method

Insight is useful only when it becomes behaviour.

Understanding and learning run side by side, meet in practice, and leave as one direction: applied behaviour.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Build awareness of yourself, others and the situation.

  2. 02

    Learn

    Acquire practical frameworks and tools you will actually use.

  3. 03

    Practise

    Rehearse in realistic exercises and facilitated practice, where it is safe to get it wrong.

  4. 04

    Apply

    Transfer the learning into specific actions at work and in everyday life.

Frameworks clarify. Practice changes behaviour.
