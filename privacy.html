Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what personal information TAO — Thrive in Life & Career ("TAO", "we") collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send us:

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, and optionally your organisation, role, area of interest and message.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address and, optionally, your first name.

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics tracking on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, to arrange a programme or workshop place.
  • To send the TAO Newsletter, if you have subscribed. Every issue lets you unsubscribe.

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Services we rely on

  • Hosting: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Fonts: typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • Forms: form submissions may be processed by a form-handling service acting on our behalf, solely to deliver your message to us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to respond and to provide any services you request. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you the newsletter. Please use the contact form and we will act on your request promptly.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
